# Risk Management

Risk management in AI is the process of identifying harm before deployment and taking steps to reduce or mitigate it. Risks can come from biased inputs, false positives, unsafe recommendations, poor user experience, or inability to explain the system's behavior to stakeholders.

A practical risk review includes evaluating failure modes, clarifying who is accountable for those outcomes, and determining whether the model should be deployed, restricted, or redesigned. Teams should also define what signals indicate a need to pause use or revert to a manual process.

Risk management fits with the broader governance lifecycle because it connects technical choices to real-world consequences. When organizations treat AI risk as a static checklist rather than a continuous practice, they become less prepared for operational drift and user feedback.
