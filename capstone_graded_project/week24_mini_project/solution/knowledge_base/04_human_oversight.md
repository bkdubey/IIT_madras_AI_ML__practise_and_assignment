# Human Oversight

Human oversight is important because AI can produce plausible but wrong outputs. Even when a model performs well on average, there may be edge cases or unusual inputs that require a human reviewer to intervene.

High-impact decisions should remain under human authority. A common practice is to require a person to confirm critical actions such as rejecting a loan application, removing a user account, or making a hiring recommendation. This review process should include clear escalation paths and accountability for final decisions.

Human oversight is not limited to approvals at the end of a workflow. Teams should provide error-reporting channels, periodic audits, and documented sign-off procedures for model updates or drift detection. This keeps the organization accountable for the AI system's downstream consequences.
